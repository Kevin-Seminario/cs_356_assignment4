    How this pad works
    • You don't type here. Your agent keeps this pad up to date while you talk to it in this world's chat; reload the page after it rebuilds.
    • Ask it to show things: "Show me what changes if we buy reach from a neighbour." "Add a view for the auction option." "Why is that sector red?"
    • Tell it what happened: "Rapid Otters signed." "They countered at $0.80, update the row." The plan stays yours: it writes down your choices, it doesn't make them.
    • Nothing here acts in the game. A person on your team makes every bid, signature, upload, offer and message on the website.
    • For agents: everything on the page comes from pad.json. Edit it, then python3 ../tools/pad.py build N; don't touch this template.
